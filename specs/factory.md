# Factory
- Factory mode is exactly `active`, `shadow`, or `paused`; missing/ambiguous means paused, and automation cannot activate itself.
- Autonomous work requires one open human-approved goal, eligible leaf issues, bounded exact paths, owned expiring claims, closed dependencies, budgets, evidence, rollback, and stop conditions.
- Risk is the maximum declared/path-derived tier; ambiguity is boundary. Canonical routing is `.github/agentic-policy.json`.
- Routine/sensitive same-repository PRs may use native auto-merge only after current-head CI, migrations, code review, conditional specialist reviews, labels, scope, and trusted-review checks pass.
- Boundary and forked PRs require current trusted human approval; agents never auto-repair or auto-merge boundary work, and stale evidence or a human veto fails closed.
- Self-heal is capped, exact-head, same-PR, and cannot weaken tests, reviews, safety, authority, or coverage.
- Autonomous deployment is limited to the exact merged SHA in development/shadow; production, secrets/accounts, architecture acceptance, paper promotion, and live trading remain human-only.
